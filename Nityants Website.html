About Me

Hi, I'm Nityant Singhal, born on 20th January 2009 and raised in Pune. I’ve lived here all my life and I have a twin brother, Hemang. I’m an athlete at heart and currently preparing for IIT-JEE while building my skills in coding and entrepreneurship.

My Sports Journey

Skating

Started at age 3. My very first sport that built my balance and agility.

Football

Joined in early school years. Loved the energy and teamwork it brought.

Basketball

Fast-paced and thrilling, basketball helped improve my reflexes.

Cricket

Played for 7–8 years and earned multiple accolades. Scored two centuries (177 & 165). Cricket taught me how to work hard, stay devoted, and push myself beyond limits.

Swimming

First attempted at age 4 but couldn't learn due to fear of water depth. After leaving cricket, I mastered swimming over 4–5 months. Now I flex it on vacations and enjoy it regularly.

Martial Arts & Fitness

In the last two years, I’ve picked up self-defense skills and basic martial arts. I currently run every morning at 7 AM and swim in the evening to stay physically and mentally sharp.

Goals & Dreams

I dream of becoming an entrepreneur who builds impactful products and businesses.

My short-term goal is to clear IIT-JEE and study at IIT Bombay.

Post-engineering, I aim to pursue an MBA from a good IIM and earn a CFA for financial expertise.

Alongside academics, I work on coding, reading, and self-growth daily — one step at a time.

Family & Support

My parents have always stood behind me — not just with words, but with actions.

They set up practice nets at home, got me a bowling machine, arranged personal fitness training coach, and supported every dream I've had.

They never let outside pressure affect how they raised me and my twin brother Hemang. We've been teammates in life — in sports, studies, and now dreams.

Whatever I am today, and whatever I’ll become, is deeply rooted in their belief in me.
